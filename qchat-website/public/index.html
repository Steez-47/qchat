What’s a clean way to debounce a search box?

Wait until the person stops typing, then search. A tiny helper keeps it tidy:

function debounce(fn, ms = 250) {
  let timer;
  return (...args) => {
    clearTimeout(timer);
    timer = setTimeout(() => fn(...args), ms);
  };
}

Wrap your input handler with it. Around 250 ms feels like a natural pause.

The tour

Built for the ten seconds between tasks.

01 / Selected text

Ask about what you’ve highlighted.

Select text in any app and press Ctrl Alt Q. QChat opens with it quoted and ready for your question. No copy, no paste.

02 / Snapshots

Show it what you’re looking at.

One click on the camera captures the window behind QChat and attaches it. Ask about a chart, an error, a design, anything on screen.

03 / Commands

Slash for everything.

Type / to switch between ChatGPT and Gemini, start a temporary chat, export to Markdown and more. Ctrl K opens the same menu.

04 / Files

Drag it in. Drop it on.

Drop files on the bar, paste an image or use the paperclip. Up to ten attachments a message, with previews before you send.

05 / History

Every chat, one search away.

Ctrl P searches the chats on this PC, then everything else on your account. Pick one and the whole conversation opens.

And the details

Small on screen. Thorough underneath.

  • Real glass

    The desktop really is blurred behind it, and clicks pass straight through the empty space around the bar.

  • Code and maths, rendered

    Highlighted code, typeset maths, tables and images. Hover a reply to copy or retry it.

  • Temporary chats

    Ctrl Shift N starts a chat that stays out of your history.

  • Kept on your PC

    Up to 50 chats are saved locally so they open instantly, and unsent drafts survive a restart.

  • Six-second undo

    Removing a chat, /clear and /discard can all be undone for six seconds.

  • Copy and export

    /copy puts the conversation on your clipboard as Markdown. /export saves it to Downloads.

  • Open at startup

    Starts quietly in the tray when you log in, so Alt Q works from the first minute.

  • Recovers by itself

    A dropped connection retries when the network returns, with your draft and files kept exactly as they were.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an API key?

No. QChat uses the ChatGPT or Gemini account you sign in with, in a hidden window, so you get that account’s models and history. Without signing in it chats as a ChatGPT guest.

What does it cost?

QChat itself is free. Your ChatGPT or Gemini plan’s own limits still apply, the same as on their websites.

Where are my chats stored?

Your conversations live in your ChatGPT or Gemini account as usual. QChat also keeps up to 50 recent chats on this PC so they open instantly, and you can turn local history off. Temporary chats stay out of both.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists SHA256 checksums if you’d like to verify the file first.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. QChat runs on Windows 11 and Windows 10 22H2, x64.

Is QChat made by OpenAI or Google?

No. It’s an independent app and isn’t affiliated with either.
