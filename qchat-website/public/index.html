Ask, then get back to work.

QChat is a tiny glass bar that chats with ChatGPT or Gemini through your own signed-in accounts. It floats over whatever you’re doing, and folds away when you’re done.

01 — Open

Press AltQ anywhere.

The orb opens into a single bar, right over the app you’re in. No browser tab, no window to manage.

02 — Ask

Ask with your own account.

QChat talks to chatgpt.com and gemini.google.com through your signed-in session, so you get your models and your history. No API keys.

03 — Read

Replies that read calmly.

Answers arrive block by block, so code, tables and maths settle in place instead of jumping around while you read.

04 — Return

Recent chats ride along.

Your last few conversations pop out of the bar as tabs, each marked with the provider it lives on.

05 — Fold

Click away. It folds.

A few seconds out of focus and everything sinks into the bar, then the bar closes into its orb. Your work is right where you left it.

What’s a clean way to debounce a search box?

Wait until the person stops typing, then search. A tiny helper keeps it tidy:

function debounce(fn, ms = 250) {
  let timer;
  return (...args) => {
    clearTimeout(timer);
    timer = setTimeout(() => fn(...args), ms);
  };
}

Wrap your input handler with it. Around 250 ms feels like a natural typing pause.

And the details

Small on screen. Thorough underneath.

  • Real glass

    The desktop really is blurred behind it, and clicks pass straight through the empty space around the bar.

  • Code and maths, rendered

    Highlighted code, typeset maths, tables and images, in the same calm bubbles. Hover a reply to copy or retry it.

  • Temporary chats

    CtrlShiftN starts a chat that stays out of history. It waits for the provider to confirm before sending.

  • Kept on your PC

    Up to 50 chats are saved locally, and unsent drafts survive a restart. Your sign-in stays saved too.

  • Six-second undo

    Removing a chat, /clear and /discard can all be undone for six seconds.

  • Copy and export

    /copy puts the conversation on your clipboard as Markdown. /export saves it to Downloads.

  • Open at startup

    Starts quietly in the tray when you log in, so AltQ works from the first minute.

  • Recovers by itself

    A dropped connection retries when the network returns, keeping your draft and files exactly as they were.

Install

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an API key?

No. QChat uses the ChatGPT or Gemini account you sign in with, in a hidden window, so you get that account’s models and history. Without signing in it chats as a ChatGPT guest.

What does it cost?

QChat itself is free. Your ChatGPT or Gemini plan’s own limits still apply, the same as on their websites.

Where are my chats stored?

Your conversations live in your ChatGPT or Gemini account as usual. QChat also keeps up to 50 recent chats on this PC so they open instantly, and you can turn local history off. Temporary chats stay out of both.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

The installer isn’t code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each release lists SHA256 checksums if you’d like to verify the file first.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. QChat runs on Windows 11 and Windows 10 22H2, x64.

Is QChat made by OpenAI or Google?

No. It’s an independent app and isn’t affiliated with either.
