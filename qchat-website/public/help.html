01

Quick start

  1. 1

    Install and launch

    Run the installer. QChat appears as a small bar near the bottom of your screen and an icon in the system tray.

  2. 2

    Sign in, once

    Open Settings → Accounts, or pick Sign in from the tray. Log in to ChatGPT or Gemini in the window that opens and you stay signed in. Without signing in, QChat chats as a ChatGPT guest.

  3. 3

    Press Alt Q from anywhere

    The bar opens over whatever you’re doing. Type, press Enter, read the reply, then click away. A few seconds later it folds back into its orb.

  4. 4

    Explore with /

    Type a slash in the bar to see every command, or press Ctrl K for the same menu. /keys lists the shortcuts.

  1. 1

    Orb

    Drag it to move QChat. Click it to fold the bar away. A ring shows the connection: none when ready, amber while connecting or as a guest, red on an error.

  2. 2

    All chats

    The clock tab opens every chat on this PC, then every chat on your account, with search. Same as Ctrl P.

  3. 3

    Recent tabs

    Your latest chats ride on the bar, each with its provider’s logo and how long ago you used it. Click one to jump back in.

  4. 4

    Composer

    Type a message, / for commands. Paste or drop files here. Up recalls earlier prompts when it’s empty.

  5. 5

    Picker

    Provider, mode (Chat, Work or Task), reasoning effort and model, all in one small popover.

  6. 6

    Snapshot

    Captures the window behind QChat and attaches it, so you can ask about what’s on screen.

  7. 7

    Attach

    Pick files to send. Up to 10 per message. Hide it in Settings → Bar & tabs if you never use it.

  8. 8

    Send

    Appears once there’s something to send. During a reply it becomes Stop.

05

Assistants & modes

QChat talks to chatgpt.com and gemini.google.com in hidden windows using your own sign-in. No API keys, and your plan’s models and limits apply as usual.

Pick a provider

The pill beside the composer opens the picker. Choose ChatGPT or Gemini at the top, or type /chatgpt or /gemini. Settings → General sets which one new chats use.

Chat, Work or Task

Work (ChatGPT) and Task (Gemini) run long agent jobs where your plan includes them. Switching starts a new chat and keeps your draft. If a task stops for your approval, QChat tells you to open the chat on the site.

Effort and model

Set reasoning effort (low, medium or high) and the model for each chat in the picker. Settings → General sets the starting effort for new chats.

Each chat keeps its provider

A chat stays with the service it started on, and its tab shows that logo. Gemini only loads the first time you use it.

06

Chats & history

  • Recent tabs. Up to three recent chats sit on the bar (Settings → Bar & tabs changes how many).
  • All chats. Ctrl P, /chats or the clock tab lists chats on this PC first, then every other chat on your account. Type to search.
  • Full history. Opening a chat loads the whole conversation from your account, images included. Long chats open at the newest message and fill in older ones above.
  • Names. New chats are named from the first line of your first prompt. /rename changes it.
  • Drafts. Unsent text survives switching chats, reloads and restarts. QChat also remembers where you were reading.
  • Temporary chats. Ctrl Shift N or /temp. Sending waits until ChatGPT confirms its Temporary mode; if it refuses, your draft and files are kept. ChatGPT only, because Gemini gives no reliable sign that a chat is temporary.
  • Undo. Removing a chat, /clear and /discard can each be undone for six seconds, or with Ctrl Z.
  • Stored locally. Up to 50 chats are kept on this PC, with full transcripts for the newest eight. Settings → Privacy turns local history off.

07

Reading replies

  • Calm streaming. Each block appears once it’s finished, then flows in word by word, so code, tables and maths never jump while you read.
  • Rich formatting. Highlighted code, typeset maths, aligned tables, task lists, images, audio and video.
  • Hover actions. Hover a reply to copy it, retry it or open it on the provider’s site. Your own messages have Copy and Edit.
  • Following along. The view follows new text until you scroll up one notch, then stays put. Reach the end and it follows again.
  • Find. Ctrl F searches the whole conversation, not just what’s on screen.
  • Take it with you. /copy puts the conversation on the clipboard as Markdown; /export saves it to Downloads.

08

Files, images & snapshots

  • Three ways in. The paperclip, pasting, or dropping files anywhere on QChat. Pasting outside a text field still goes to the composer.
  • Previews. Images show thumbnails before you send and previews in the chat. Copy image puts the pixels on the clipboard.
  • Snapshots. The camera button captures the window behind QChat and attaches it. Great for errors, charts and designs.
  • Ask about selected text. Select text in any app and press Ctrl Alt Q. It appears as a one-line quote above your question. If an app won’t share its selection, QChat can copy it for you and restore your clipboard (Settings → Shortcuts).
  • Downloads. Files in replies download without overwriting anything you already have.
  • Retry. A message whose attachments failed offers Retry for ten minutes, and a file that failed to prepare has its own Retry.

09

The orb & folding

Click away and, after five seconds, everything sinks into the bar and the bar closes into its orb. Click the orb, press Alt Q or any key to open it again. Drag the folded orb to move it.

  • Fold it yourself. Click the open orb, or the minimize button shown during a reply.
  • When it won’t fold. Never while words are arriving in an ordinary reply, while the pointer is over it or while Settings is open. Long Work and Task jobs may fold at any point.
  • Change the timing. Settings → Window → Fold into the orb when idle. Set it to 0 to turn folding off, or choose Hide when you click away instead.

10

The tray icon

Right-click QChat’s icon in the notification area for:

  • Show QChat
  • New chat
  • Settings…
  • Sign in to ChatGPT…
  • Sign in to Gemini…
  • Open at startup
  • Quit

Open at startup starts QChat quietly in the tray when you log in, so Alt Q works from the first minute. Turn on Show the bar at startup in Settings → General to see it straight away. /hide or Esc only hide the bar; Quit closes QChat and its tray icon.

12

Updates

  • One click. When a new release is out, a small arrow appears on the orb. Hover it and press Update: QChat downloads the new version, checks its checksum and restarts into it.
  • Quietly. QChat only asks GitHub for its latest release, at startup and about every six hours. /update checks right now. Settings → General turns checking off.
  • Nightly. Want features early? Install QChat Nightly once and it offers newer Nightlies the same way. It shares your settings, chats and sign-in.

13

Limits & troubleshooting

Windows shows a SmartScreen warning

The installer isn’t code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release lists SHA256 checksums if you’d like to verify the file first.

The bar is off screen or in an odd spot

Settings → Window → Reset position puts it back at the bottom centre. QChat opens toward the middle of whichever screen you last left it on.

Everything looks too big or too small

Settings → Chat → Fit to this screen measures your screen again and resets only the size settings. Handy after moving to another monitor.

“Temporary chat could not be confirmed”

ChatGPT can refuse Temporary mode on guest accounts and on non-English or changed interfaces. QChat keeps your draft and files instead of sending a normal chat. That’s the safe failure.

Lost connection

A connection error retries by itself when the network returns or QChat regains focus, keeping your draft and files. You can also press Retry on the error.

Alt+Q does nothing

Another app may own the shortcut. Pick a new one in Settings → Shortcuts. Click the shortcut, then press the new combination; it needs Ctrl, Alt or Windows.

Attachment limits

Up to 10 attachments and 512 MB per message. ChatGPT’s and Gemini’s own upload and format limits still apply, and audio or video playback depends on WebView2’s codecs.

What isn’t supported yet

Live voice and camera sessions. Mac and Linux. QChat runs on Windows 11 and Windows 10 22H2, x64.
